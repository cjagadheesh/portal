# Launch video credits

`portal-launch.mp4` (47 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps) was built with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/).
`portal-launch-poster.png` is a frame from it. The terminal output in the video is illustrative,
not a recording, because nothing in this repo has run on real AWS yet.

## Narration

Generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) text-to-speech: voice "Jerry B. – Conversational
Marketing VO", model `eleven_v4`, one take per scene. Whether it can be used commercially depends on
the ElevenLabs plan of the account that generated it. The free plan is non-commercial and requires
crediting ElevenLabs, so keep the "Voice: ElevenLabs" credit wherever the video is published unless
the account is on a paid plan.

Script:

1. Your cloud dev box? Still running. Nineteen days later.
2. And nobody remembered to turn it off.
3. Meet Portal. Spot dev boxes that can't run up a surprise bill.
4. One command, and your box is ready in about a minute.
5. SSH in with no keys, and no open ports. Your edits sync in a second.
6. Four safety layers, each working alone. The last one lives in AWS, and trusts only tags.
7. Past its expiry? It's gone.
8. Done for the day? Your home volume waits for next time.
9. Portal. Spin up. Ship. Forget safely.
